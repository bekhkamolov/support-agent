# When will I get my refund?

Refunds are issued after the returned parcel arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection, which usually takes 3 to 5 business days after arrival. The money is sent back to the original payment method. Card refunds appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on the bank; PayPal refunds usually appear within 24 hours. Gift card payments are refunded as a new gift card. You will receive an email as soon as the refund has been issued.
