# Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or stops working, report it within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the damage. Damaged items are not counted against the 30-day return window. We offer a free replacement or a full refund, including the original shipping costs. For electronics, the statutory warranty of 2 years applies in addition; after 14 days, defects are handled as warranty claims (see "Warranty for electronics").
