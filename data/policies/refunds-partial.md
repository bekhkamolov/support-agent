# Partial refunds and deductions

If a returned item shows signs of use, missing tags, or damage caused by the customer, we may issue a partial refund of up to 50 percent of the item price after inspection. Return label costs for non-free countries (EUR 6.95) are deducted from the refund. Discount codes used on the original order are not refunded as cash; if the remaining order no longer meets the code's minimum value, the discount is recalculated.
